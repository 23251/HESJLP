3 directions · Custom

A mobile-first public health education website for Illinois Medicaid patients and their families. Patients reach it by scanning a QR code on a printed flyer. It must answer, fast, on a phone: Is this change about me? What do I do now? Who do I call? Where can I get care if I lose coverage? Content: key dates, work requirements, exemptions, how to keep coverage, non-citizen rules, what to do if you lose Medicaid, phone numbers, and a list of free and low-cost clinics in Cook County. Plain language at roughly a 6th-grade reading level, WCAG 2.1 AA (4.5:1 text contrast, visible focus, 44px targets), friendly and calm, not corporate. Home page is a hub: a short 'Does this apply to you?' section, then big tappable cards to each topic, then 'Who to call'. Built as plain HTML/CSS/JS. Each direction is a sketch of the home page on a phone-width and desktop-width layout.
