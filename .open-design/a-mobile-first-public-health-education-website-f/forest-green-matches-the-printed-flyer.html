Medicaid Is Changing

Some people could lose their coverage. Here's how to stay covered.

What's changing in Illinois, what to do now, and where to get help.

  • Lost Medicaid? Get careYou can still get care

Does this apply to you?

Work rules and 6-month renewals are for ACA Adults: people with Medicaid through the ACA (Obamacare).

Most are 19 to 64, aren't on Medicare, and don't have a child under 18 living with them.

Not sure?

Check State letters for your “medical group,” ask your health plan, or go to Manage My Case:

  1. Case Summary
  2. Benefit Details
  3. Notices

Key dates

  • Oct. 1, 2026Some non-citizens lost federally funded Medicaid.
  • Dec. 31, 2026Work rules apply to new applications made after 5 p.m.
  • Jan. 1, 2027Work rules and 6-month renewals begin (first renewals: March).

3 things you can do

  1. Open all mail from the State

    Letters tell you what you need to do and by when.

  2. Keep your address and phone up to date

    Tell HFS right away if anything changes.

  3. Renew on time

    Starting in 2027, some adults must renew every 6 months and show work, school, or volunteer hours.
