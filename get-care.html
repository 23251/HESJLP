Emergency?

Go to the nearest ER

  • Every hospital ER must treat emergencies, even if you have no insurance or can't pay.
  • Can't get regular Medicaid? Emergency Medicaid (ages 19+) may pay the ER bill.
  • The hospital can apply for Emergency Medicaid for you.

Sources: Illinois law (210 ILCS 70), Illinois law (210 ILCS 80), HFS non-citizen toolkit

Check-ups and sick visits

Community health centers

  • Neighborhood clinics for check-ups and sick visits. You pay based on your income.
  • No one is turned away for not being able to pay. Any immigration status is welcome.

See the clinic list

More free clinics: illinoisfreeclinics.org

Source: City of Chicago health center flyer

Cook County Health's discount program

CareLink

  • Up to 100% off care at Cook County's public hospitals and clinics.
  • For uninsured adults 19–64 in Cook County 30+ days, any immigration status.
  • Bring ID and proof of income and address.

Call (866) 223-2817

Sources: Cook County Health: CareLink, Cook County immigrant resources

Got a hospital bill?

Ask for financial assistance

No insurance and live in Illinois? Ask the hospital for help before you pay.

  • Earn up to about $31,900 a year (more for families)? Free care at most hospitals.
  • Earn up to about $95,700 a year (more for families)? You can get a discount.

Sources: Illinois law (210 ILCS 89), U.S. poverty guidelines (HHS)

Kids, pregnancy, or a new green card

  • Under 19 or pregnant? You may get coverage through All Kids or Moms & Babies.
  • Got a green card? You may qualify for Medicaid again.

Call Illinois Medicaid (HFS): 1-877-805-5312

Sources: HFS non-citizen guide, SHVS immigrant eligibility brief

Who lost Medicaid

Refugees, asylees, TPS, parolees (including Ukrainian and Afghan), and trafficking/domestic violence survivors without a green card, among others. Getting a green card may restore Medicaid.

Sources: HFS non-citizen toolkit, HFS non-citizen notice
