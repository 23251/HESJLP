Medicaid changes and work rules

  • Illinois HFS: Medicaid ChangesWork rules, key dates, 6-month renewals, rules for non-citizens, promising you are exempt, the Manage My Case steps, and the HFS phone number.
  • HFS: Notice to ACA Adults (Sept. 1, 2026, PDF)Who is an ACA Adult, the full list of exemptions, what counts as work or school, the 30 days to send information, and no help with premiums after losing Medicaid for work rules. Also the exemption screener coming in late 2026 and the ABE Customer Service number and TTY.
  • HFS: H.R. 1 Phase 2 Toolkit (PDF)Who is an ACA Adult, 6-month renewals from Jan. 1, 2027, 1 month in every 6, $580 or 80 hours, and letters mailed in Sept. 2026.
  • HFS: H.R. 1 Module 4, Exemptions from Work Requirements (draft, PDF)Even exempt ACA Adults must renew every 6 months. Details on each exemption: special medical needs, caregivers, pregnancy, former foster youth, veterans, and short-term hardship.
  • Get Medicaid Facts (Cook County Health)First renewals in March 2027. New applicants are checked on the month before they apply.
  • HFS: Medicaid FAQsFirst renewals in March 2027. Who the work rules don't apply to, SNAP counting for Medicaid, the 30 days to respond, and 60 days to appeal.
  • Stateline: Court ruling on "medically frail" (Oct. 2026)On Oct. 29, 2026, a federal judge ruled that people with special medical needs don't also have to prove they can't work.
  • Illinois Attorney General: Lawsuit over work rules (June 29, 2026)Illinois is one of the states that sued over the "medically frail" rule.

Non-citizens

  • HFS: Non-citizen coverage notice (June 26, 2026, PDF)Which non-citizens keep Medicaid. Coverage for others ended Sept. 30, 2026.
  • HFS: Non-citizen toolkit (PDF)Who lost and who kept Medicaid on Oct. 1, 2026, and Emergency Medicaid (ages 19+, the hospital can apply).
  • HFS: H.R. 1 Module 2, Non-citizen Eligibility Changes (draft, PDF)Veterans and active duty keep coverage only with a green card. Illinois still covers all children under 19 and pregnant people. Emergency Medicaid for ages 19+.
  • CMS: Guidance on non-citizen eligibility, SHO #26-001 (Apr. 8, 2026, PDF)The federal rules for non-citizens starting Oct. 1, 2026.
  • SHVS / Manatt: H.R. 1 immigrant eligibility changesGetting a green card later can make you eligible again if you meet the other rules.

Emergency care and hospital bills

  • Illinois law: Emergency Medical Treatment Act (210 ILCS 70)No one can be refused needed emergency treatment because they can't pay.
  • Illinois law: Hospital Emergency Service Act (210 ILCS 80)Hospitals must check and stabilize anyone who comes to the ER.
  • Illinois law: Hospital Uninsured Patient Discount Act (210 ILCS 89)Free hospital care and discounts for uninsured patients, by income.
  • U.S. HHS: 2026 Poverty GuidelinesThe yearly income amounts for free care (about $31,900) and discounts (about $95,700) for 1 person.
