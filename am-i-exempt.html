Work rules may not apply

You may not be an ACA Adult

Work rules are only for ACA Adults. If you're under 19 or over 64, have Medicare, have a related child under 18 living with you, or are in another Medicaid group, they likely don't apply to you.

  • Check your medical group in State letters, or in Manage My Case: Case Summary → Benefit Details → Notices.
  • Think HFS has you in the wrong group? Call ABE Customer Service.

1-800-843-6154 TTY: 1-866-324-5553

Sources: HFS letter to ACA Adults, HFS Medicaid FAQs

Find out your Medicaid group

Your group decides if the work rules apply to you.

  • Or check State letters for your "medical group," or ask your health plan.
  • Or call Illinois Medicaid (HFS).

May be exempt

Health conditions ("special medical needs")

Not sure if your condition counts? You don't have to figure it out alone. The steps below can help you find out.

People with special medical needs (HFS calls this "medically frail") don't have to meet work rules. HFS lists these groups:

  • Blind or disabled under Social Security standards
  • A drug or alcohol use condition
  • A serious mental health condition
  • A physical, intellectual, or developmental disability that makes daily activities hard
  • A serious or complex medical condition that needs regular treatment

HFS decides if your condition counts. It will check information it already has first. If it can't confirm, it may ask you for more information.

These rules are changing. On Oct. 29, 2026, a federal judge ruled that people in these groups don't also have to prove they can't work. Illinois was one of the states that sued. HFS hasn't updated its guidance yet, so check back.

What to do

  • Use the screener at abe.illinois.gov: "Check to see if I meet SNAP & Medicaid Work Requirements." It can help you ask for an exemption.
  • Ask your doctor or clinic about your condition, and keep copies of records in case HFS asks.
  • Questions about your coverage? Call Illinois Medicaid (HFS).

1-877-805-5312

  • Need free legal help? Use Illinois Legal Aid Online's Get Legal Help.
  • Living with HIV? AIDS Foundation Chicago helps with insurance questions (bilingual, Mon–Fri 8:30–5:30).

312-784-9060

Sources: HFS letter to ACA Adults, HFS exemptions guide, Stateline: court ruling, Illinois Attorney General, Illinois Legal Aid Online: Get Legal Help, AIDS Foundation of Chicago

May be exempt

In drug or alcohol treatment

People in a program to treat drug or alcohol use don't have to meet work rules. A drug or alcohol use condition can also count as a special medical need.

  • Use the screener at abe.illinois.gov to ask for an exemption.
  • Ask your treatment program for a letter or records in case HFS asks.

Sources: HFS letter to ACA Adults, HFS Medicaid FAQs

May be exempt

Short-term hardship

A hospital, rehab, or psychiatric hospital stay, or traveling far for medical care you can't get nearby, can count. You must ask for this exemption.

HFS gives this exemption on its own if you live in a county with a federal disaster or high unemployment. It will tell you.

  • Use the screener at abe.illinois.gov, or call ABE Customer Service.

1-800-843-6154 TTY: 1-866-324-5553

Sources: HFS letter to ACA Adults, HFS Medicaid FAQs

May be exempt

Pregnant or recently pregnant

If you're pregnant, or your pregnancy ended in the last 12 months (including a miscarriage), you don't have to meet work rules. This includes getting coverage after pregnancy through Moms & Babies.

  • Tell HFS you're pregnant. Call Illinois Medicaid (HFS).

1-877-805-5312

Sources: HFS letter to ACA Adults, HFS exemptions guide

May be exempt

Caregivers

Parents, guardians, relatives, and family caregivers (paid or unpaid) of a child under 14 or a person with a disability don't have to meet work rules.

  • Use the screener at abe.illinois.gov to ask for an exemption, or call ABE Customer Service.

1-800-843-6154 TTY: 1-866-324-5553

Sources: HFS letter to ACA Adults, HFS exemptions guide

May be exempt

Former foster youth under 26

If you had Medicaid while in foster care and you're under 26, you don't have to meet work rules. It doesn't matter which state you were in foster care in.

  • Use the screener at abe.illinois.gov, or call Illinois Medicaid (HFS).

1-877-805-5312

Sources: HFS letter to ACA Adults, HFS exemptions guide

May be exempt

Veterans rated 100% disabled

Veterans with a VA disability rating of 100% don't have to meet work rules.

  • Check your rating on the VA disability rating page.
  • Then use the screener at abe.illinois.gov to ask for an exemption.

Sources: HFS letter to ACA Adults, HFS exemptions guide, VA: View your disability ratings

May be exempt

American Indian or Alaska Native

American Indians, Alaska Natives, American Urban Indians, California Indians, members of a recognized tribe, and people who can get care through the Indian Health Service don't have to meet work rules.

  • Make sure HFS knows. Call Illinois Medicaid (HFS).

1-877-805-5312

Source: HFS letter to ACA Adults

May be exempt

In jail or prison, or recently released

If you're in jail or prison now, or got out in the last 3 months, you don't have to meet work rules.

  • Keep your release papers. Call Illinois Medicaid (HFS) with questions.

1-877-805-5312

Sources: HFS letter to ACA Adults, HFS exemptions guide

May be exempt

Getting TANF

If you get TANF and follow its work rules, you don't have to meet Medicaid's work rules too.

  • Questions? Call ABE Customer Service.

1-800-843-6154 TTY: 1-866-324-5553

Sources: HFS letter to ACA Adults, HFS exemptions guide

May be exempt

Getting SNAP

Meeting SNAP's work rules, or being in SNAP's 3-month grace period, counts for Medicaid. HFS marks you exempt on its own. A SNAP exemption may not count for Medicaid.

  • Check your SNAP status in Manage My Case at abe.illinois.gov, or call ABE Customer Service.

1-800-843-6154 TTY: 1-866-324-5553

Sources: HFS letter to ACA Adults, HFS Medicaid FAQs

Work rules may apply

You may need to meet the work rules

If you're an ACA Adult and none of these fit you, do ONE of these for at least 1 month out of every 6:

  • Earn $580 or more in the month
  • Go to school at least half-time
  • Do 80 hours of work, volunteering, school, or a work program in a month
  • Find work programs at snapworkrequirements.illinois.gov.
  • If things change, like you get sick or start caring for someone, you can ask for an exemption then.
  • Read more about the work rules

Sources: HFS work rules toolkit, HFS Medicaid FAQs, HFS letter to ACA Adults

For everyone, even if you're exempt

  • ACA Adults must renew every 6 months, even if exempt. Work rules start at your first renewal on or after Jan. 1, 2027 (first renewals: March). New applications after 5 p.m. on Dec. 31, 2026 must meet them too.
  • Read State mail right away. If HFS asks for more information, you have 30 days to send it. You're still covered during that time.
  • Keep proof of your hours or exemption.
  • HFS says the abe.illinois.gov screener and exemption requests are coming in late 2026. Can't find them yet? Call ABE Customer Service at 1-800-843-6154 (TTY 1-866-324-5553).
  • If HFS can't confirm you're exempt in 2027, you can promise that you are. (Lying about it is a crime.)
  • Think a decision is wrong? You have 60 days to appeal. Call Medicaid Appeals:

1-800-435-0774

TTY: 1-877-734-7429
